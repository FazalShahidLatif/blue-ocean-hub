## Logo

**File:** `public/logo.svg`  
**Favicon:** `public/favicon.svg`  
**OG Image:** `public/og-image.jpg`

The logo is a compass rose with ocean waves, representing navigation and strategic direction. The compass needle points northeast (growth direction). Ocean waves represent the "Blue Ocean" concept of uncontested market space.

### Logo Usage

- Always use `logo.svg` for the brand mark
- Minimum size: 32×32px
- Clear space: equal to the height of the compass circle on all sides
- Do not rotate, recolor, or modify the logo
- On dark backgrounds: use as-is (cyan on transparent)
- On light backgrounds: use with `filter: brightness(0) saturate(100%) invert(18%) sepia(15%) saturate(2000%) hue-rotate(180deg)`

## Brand Voice

- **Tone:** Institutional, authoritative, educational
- **Style:** Short sentences, data-driven, actionable
- **Avoid:** Hype, jargon, personality-driven content
- **Focus:** Strategic utility, capital preservation, systematic wealth protection
