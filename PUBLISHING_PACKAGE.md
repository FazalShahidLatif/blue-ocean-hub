## Article 1: How to open a CDC account and buy your first stock in Pakistan

### Quora (Answer format)
**Question:** How do I open a CDC account and buy my first stock in Pakistan?
**Answer:**
Pakistan has one of the lowest stock-market participation rates in the world. Fewer than 2% of adults own shares, while inflation quietly erodes the rupee value of every rupee left in a savings account. Opening a stock market account takes about 15 minutes, costs nothing upfront, and gives you direct ownership of profitable Pakistani companies.

**Step 1: Choose a broker (TREC holder)**
You need a broker registered with the Pakistan Stock Exchange. Top options: Arif Habib Limited, AKD Securities, Topline Securities, Intermarket Securities. Compare commission rates (typically 0.15-0.25% per trade) and platform quality.

**Step 2: Submit documents**
- CNIC (front and back)
- Bank account statement (last 3 months)
- Proof of income (salary slip or tax return)
- Passport-size photo

**Step 3: CDC account creation**
Your broker handles CDC account creation. The CDC (Central Depository Company) maintains the electronic register of your share ownership. You'll receive a CDC sub-account number.

**Step 4: Fund your account**
Transfer money from your bank to your broker's designated account. Minimum investment varies by broker (typically Rs 5,000-25,000).

**Step 5: Place your first trade**
Use your broker's trading platform (web or mobile app) to place a buy order. Start with a large-cap stock like OGDC, HBL, or MCB for your first trade.

**Key risks to understand:**
- Market risk: stocks can drop 20-30% in a bad year
- Liquidity risk: some stocks have low trading volume
- Currency risk: if you earn in PKR, your real returns depend on inflation

**Tax treatment:** Capital gains on shares held for more than 1 year are taxed at 12.5%. Dividends are subject to 15% withholding tax.

## Article 2: 10 Passive Income Ideas for Salaried Employees in Pakistan (2026)

### Quora
**Question:** What are the best passive income ideas for salaried employees in Pakistan?
**Answer:**
Most salaried workers in Pakistan save less than 5% of their income — not because they earn too little, but because nobody taught them what to do with the money they do save.

Here are 10 passive income ideas specifically for the Pakistani economy:

1. **Dividend-paying stocks on PSX** — 47 dividend-paying stocks listed. Start with Rs 10,000 in a large-cap like HBL or MCB.

2. **Real estate investment trusts (REITs)** — Invest in property without buying property. Dolmen City REIT is a popular option.

3. **National Savings Certificates** — Government-backed, 10-14% returns. Available at post offices and banks.

4. **Freelancing on the side** — Use your existing skills on Upwork or Fiverr. Even 5 hours/week can generate $200-500/month.

5. **Digital products** — Create templates, guides, or courses. Sell on Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy.

6. **Affiliate marketing** — Recommend tools you use. TravelPayouts, Wise, Payoneer all have affiliate programs.

7. **YouTube channel** — Create content in your expertise area. Monetize with ads after 1,000 subscribers.

8. **Rental income** — If you own property, rent it out. Or use Airbnb for short-term rentals.

9. **Peer-to-peer lending** — Lend to verified borrowers through platforms like Tez Financial.

10. **SIP in mutual funds** — Invest Rs 1,000/month in a balanced fund. Let compounding work over 5+ years.

The key principle: start small, automate, and let time do the work. Don't try all 10 at once — pick 2-3 that match your skills and capital.
